HUONG DAN SU DUNG VNSTOCK APP
================================

APP NAY DUNG DE LAM GI?
- Tra cuu gia co phieu, bao cao tai chinh, thong tin cong ty cua
  cac ma chung khoan Viet Nam (Tab 1).
- Thong ke 1 hoac nhieu chi tieu tai chinh cho TOAN BO cong ty
  trong 1 nganh nghe, xem duoc nhieu nam cung luc (Tab 2 - MOI).
- Ban chi can go tu khoa va bam nut, khong can biet code.

--------------------------------------------------
BUOC 1: CHUAN BI
--------------------------------------------------
1. Dam bao may da cai Python (ban co the da cai roi vi truoc do
   da chay lenh "pip install vnstock" thanh cong).
   Neu chua co Python: tai tai https://www.python.org/downloads/
   Khi cai, nho tick chon "Add Python to PATH".

2. Copy toan bo thu muc "vnstock_app" nay vao mot noi de nho,
   vi du: D:\vnstock_app

--------------------------------------------------
BUOC 2: MO APP
--------------------------------------------------
- Vao thu muc vnstock_app, double-click vao file:

      Mo_App.bat

- Lan dau chay se tu dong kiem tra va cai thu vien vnstock neu
  chua co (co the mat 1-2 phut). Cac lan sau se mo nhanh hon.

- Mot cua so den (Command Prompt) se hien ra de xu ly, ke tiep
  la giao dien app hien ra, co 2 tab tren cung. KHONG DONG cua
  so den do trong luc dung app - do la noi hien thong bao loi
  chi tiet neu co van de.

--------------------------------------------------
TAB 1: TRA CUU 1 MA CO PHIEU
--------------------------------------------------
1. Nhap MA CO PHIEU, vi du: VNM, FPT, ACB, VCB, DHG, IMP, DBD...
2. Neu muon xem gia lich su, chon them khoang ngay.
3. Chon "Ky bao cao": year (nam) hoac quarter (quy).
4. Bam mot trong 6 nut chuc nang (Gia lich su, Thong tin cong ty,
   Bang can doi ke toan, Ket qua kinh doanh, Luu chuyen tien te,
   Chi so tai chinh).
5. Du lieu hien ra trong bang o giua man hinh.
6. Bam "Xuat ra Excel" hoac "Xuat ra CSV" o duoi cung de luu.

--------------------------------------------------
TAB 2: THONG KE THEO NGANH (TINH NANG MOI)
--------------------------------------------------
Dung khi ban muon so sanh 1 chi tieu tai chinh giua NHIEU cong ty
cung nganh, qua nhieu nam.

Cach dung:
1. O "Nganh nghe": go ten nganh, vi du:
      Duoc pham
      Ngan hang
      Bat dong san
   (khong can go dau chinh xac, app tu tim gan giong)

2. O "Chi tieu": go ten chi tieu trong bao cao KQKD ma ban muon
   lay, vi du:
      Doanh thu thuan
   Neu muon lay nhieu chi tieu cung luc, phan cach bang dau phay:
      Doanh thu thuan, Loi nhuan sau thue, Loi nhuan gop

3. Chon "Ky bao cao": year hoac quarter.

4. "Toi da so cong ty xu ly": mac dinh 30. Neu nganh co nhieu
   cong ty va ban muon lay het, tang so nay len (vi du 100).
   So lon hon se chay lau hon (moi cong ty can 1 lan goi du lieu).

5. Bam "Thong ke theo nganh". App se:
   - Tim danh sach cong ty trong nganh do
   - Lay bao cao KQKD cua tung cong ty
   - Tim chi tieu ban yeu cau trong tung bao cao
   - Ghep lai thanh 1 bang: Ma CP | Ten cong ty | Chi tieu | 2021 | 2022 | 2023...

6. Ket qua hien trong bang, xuat ra Excel de xem/phan tich them.

Luu y:
- Qua trinh nay co the mat vai chuc giay den vai phut tuy so
  luong cong ty, vi phai lay du lieu tung cong ty mot.
- Dong trang thai (mau xanh, phia tren bang) se bao dang xu ly
  cong ty nao, den dau roi.
- Neu 1 vai cong ty khong lay duoc du lieu (vi du: moi len san,
  bao cao khong day du), app se tu bo qua va van tra ve ket qua
  cho cac cong ty con lai. Chi tiet cac truong hop bi bo qua duoc
  in trong cua so den (Command Prompt).
- Ten chi tieu ban go khong can trung 100% - app tu do tim dong co
  TEN CHI TIEU chua tu khoa ban go (vi du go "doanh thu thuan" se
  khop dong "Doanh thu thuan ve ban hang va cung cap dich vu").
  Neu khong tim thay, thu go tu khoa ngan hon hoac khac di.
- Neu go sai ten nganh, thong bao loi se hien mot vai ten nganh
  co thuc trong du lieu de ban tham khao va go lai cho dung.

--------------------------------------------------
CAC LOI THUONG GAP
--------------------------------------------------
- "Chua co thu vien vnstock": Mo_App.bat se tu cai, cho vai phut.
  Neu van loi, mo Command Prompt (CMD) go tay:
      pip install -U vnstock

- "Khong lay duoc du lieu": kiem tra lai ma co phieu/tu khoa co
  dung khong, va may co dang ket noi Internet khong.

- Tab 2 bao "Khong xac dinh duoc cot nganh...": co nghia la phien
  ban vnstock hien tai tra ve du lieu voi cau truc khac so voi du
  kien. Chup lai thong bao loi day du (bao gom danh sach "Cac cot
  co san") va gui lai de duoc dieu chinh app.

- Cua so app khong hien ra: thu mo lai Mo_App.bat, doc ky thong
  bao loi trong cua so den (mau do) va gui lai cho nguoi ho tro.

--------------------------------------------------
LUU Y
--------------------------------------------------
- Du lieu do vnstock cung cap chi phuc vu tham khao/nghien cuu,
  KHONG dung de ra quyet dinh giao dich khi chua kiem tra lai.
- Vnstock co giay phep danh cho ca nhan/nghien cuu, khong dung
  cho muc dich thuong mai neu chua duoc tac gia cho phep.
